## Cultural-safety review

The stewardship dashboard does not rank Units or users, calculate success rates, hide historical work, or penalize unsuccessful, inconclusive, superseded, or cancelled efforts. “Problems Addressed” is explicitly limited to Problems with recorded Unit Solution Efforts and does not imply Unit affectedness. Failed Approaches and unsuccessful outcomes remain visible institutional contributions.

## Reporting-burden review

The architecture follows the intended flow: Project teams maintain Projects and Updates; FORGE derives the Unit portfolio; Unit Administrators manage responsibility, continuity, scoped users, exceptions, Unit POC, and data quality. No duplicate Unit status report or manually maintained knowledge summary was added.

## Pilot-critical remaining gaps

No remaining gap materially blocks the controlled Unit Administrator pilot. Production identity, notifications, and deployment infrastructure remain outside this pass as directed.

## Future enhancements

- Relational parent-command hierarchy and inherited administration.
- Lightweight receiving-Unit acknowledgment for Lead Unit transfers.
- Unit portfolio export and Unit-level AI handoff.
- External notifications and automated stewardship reminders.
- Advanced neutral analytics without rankings or success-rate incentives.
- Governed editing for approved Unit description, general location, and capability tags.
